# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Using SQL

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes pizza sales data using **SQL** to uncover meaningful business insights related to sales performance, customer ordering patterns, pizza preferences, revenue contribution, and peak ordering hours.

The goal of this analysis is to use SQL to transform raw sales data into actionable insights that can help a pizza business understand its customers and improve sales and operational decisions.

## 🔍 Key Business Questions

The analysis answers questions such as:

* What are the total orders and total revenue?
* What is the average number of orders per day?
* Which pizza is the most expensive?
* Which pizza size is ordered most frequently?
* What are the top 5 most ordered pizzas?
* Which pizza categories perform the best?
* Which pizzas generate the most revenue?
* What percentage of revenue does each category contribute?
* During which hours are orders highest?
* Which pizza sizes and categories have the lowest demand?

## 📊 Key Insights

* **Total Orders:** 21,350
* **Total Revenue:** 817,860
* **Average Orders per Day:** 138
* **Highest-Priced Pizza:** Greek Pizza — 35.95
* **Most Ordered Size:** Large (L)
* **Least Ordered Size:** Double XXL

### 🏆 Top 5 Most Ordered Pizzas

1. Classic Deluxe
2. Barbecue Chicken
3. Hawaiian Pizza
4. Pepperoni Pizza
5. Thai Chicken Pizza

### 💰 Top 3 Pizzas by Revenue

1. Thai Chicken Pizza
2. Barbecue Chicken Pizza
3. California Chicken Pizza

### 📈 Category Performance

* **Most Ordered Category:** Classic
* **Least Ordered Category:** Chicken
* **Highest Revenue Contribution:** Classic — 26.91%
* **Lowest Revenue Contribution:** Chicken — 23.96%

### ⏰ Peak Ordering Hours

The highest order volumes were observed around:

* 12 PM
* 1 PM
* 5 PM
* 6 PM
* 7 PM

After these peak hours, order volume starts to decline.

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis shows that **Classic pizzas and Large-sized pizzas have strong customer demand**. However, the pizzas with the highest order volume are not exactly the same as those generating the highest revenue.

This highlights the importance of analyzing both **order quantity and revenue** when evaluating product performance.

The concentration of orders around lunch and evening hours can also help the business optimize **staffing, inventory, and promotional strategies** during peak periods.
